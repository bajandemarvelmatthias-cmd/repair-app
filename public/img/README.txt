Photo slots for the customer dashboard (optional).
Until you add files, the page shows a built-in illustration / device icon.

  public/img/hero.jpg          Overview banner photo (right side of the hero box)
  public/img/guides/<id>.jpg   Photo for each repair guide, where <id> is the guide's id
                               (guide 1 -> guides/1.jpg, guide 2 -> guides/2.jpg ...)

Export them from Figma as JPG, roughly 1600x900 for guides and 1200x1100 for the hero.
